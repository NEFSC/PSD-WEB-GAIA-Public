About Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds an extra layer of security to your account. Even if someone knows your password, they won't be able to access your account without the second factor - a time-based code from your authenticator app.

Supported Authentication Methods

  • Authenticator Apps: Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy, 1Password, and any TOTP-compatible app
  • Hardware Keys: YubiKey devices for the strongest possible security
